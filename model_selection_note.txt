Final Model Selection: Tuned Lasso Regression

Tuned Lasso was retained as the deployed model because it achieved
the lowest RMSE (47.692) and highest R-squared (0.373) among the
models in the recorded comparison.

Tuned Ridge achieved the lowest MAE (18.870), compared with Lasso's
MAE of 20.166. Therefore, Lasso is not the best model on every metric.

The final choice reflects the preference for RMSE and R-squared in
this comparison. Its test-set performance is modest, so further
validation and improvement are recommended before real-world use.
